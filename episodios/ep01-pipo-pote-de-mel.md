# Episódio 1: Pipo e o Pote de Mel (Short de ~30 s, 9:16)

Modelo sugerido: Seedance 2.0 Mini no Higgsfield (8 s, cerca de 8 créditos por clipe). Os prompts também funcionam no Wan2GP.

## Clipe 1: Pipo acorda e sente cheiro de mel
Pixar-style 3D animation, vertical 9:16. A small orange plush teddy bear cub with big shiny eyes and a blue knitted scarf wakes up in a cozy pastel kid's bedroom, morning sunlight through the window. He sniffs the air, eyes widen with delight, and he smiles toward a glowing honey jar on a high shelf. Slow push-in camera to his face, shallow depth of field, soft warm lighting, gentle cheerful mood, no text on screen.

## Clipe 2: Pipo não alcança o pote
Pixar-style 3D animation, vertical 9:16. A small orange plush teddy bear cub with big shiny eyes and a blue knitted scarf stands on tiptoes in a cozy pastel kitchen, stretching his little arms toward a honey jar on a high wooden shelf, wobbling and almost reaching it, then plopping softly onto his bottom with a surprised face. Static medium shot at character height, soft warm lighting, gentle physical comedy, no text on screen.

## Clipe 3: Lila chega e ajuda
Pixar-style 3D animation, vertical 9:16. A small white fluffy plush bunny with big shiny eyes and a pink ribbon bow hops into a cozy pastel kitchen, sees a small orange plush teddy bear cub with a blue knitted scarf looking sad under a high shelf, and together they stack three colorful cushions into a wobbly tower. Camera slowly rises with the tower, soft warm lighting, playful teamwork mood, no text on screen.

## Clipe 4: Os dois dividem o mel
Pixar-style 3D animation, vertical 9:16. A small orange plush teddy bear cub with a blue knitted scarf and a small white fluffy plush bunny with a pink ribbon bow sit side by side on the kitchen floor sharing a honey jar, honey on their noses, laughing and hugging. Gentle side tracking shot, golden afternoon light, warm happy ending, no text on screen.
